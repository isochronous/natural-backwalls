# Changelog

## Unreleased

- First version: natural backwall tiles in every biome, built from the Aquatic Planet Pack's own worldgen data (backwall noise plus a per-biome element band). One row per biome family: a material dropdown offering that biome's solids, a coverage text box (default 0.4, or 0.15 for the denser kelp forest pattern), plus a Patterns section with a dropdown per subworld zone (the pack's reef, kelp forest, abyss and beach trees shipped with the mod, plus the base game's Strange), plus one starting-biome coverage (default 1) that applies to whatever biome the colony starts in. The Aquatic Planet Pack's own biomes are listed with their vanilla values as defaults. Backwalls on Spaced Out's other planetoids are optional and off by default. Feature rooms (carved caves, lakes, geodes), which the game's own pass leaves bare, get backwalls too (optional, on by default). A per-zone backwall census is logged at every world load. An options button exports the settings as a worldgen YAML overlay for external renderers. Biomes from other worldgen mods get their most common solid (optional); all patches are guarded so a failure leaves worldgen untouched. Surface and space subworlds stay open. New worlds only.
