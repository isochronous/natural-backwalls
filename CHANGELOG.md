# Changelog

## Unreleased

- First version: natural backwall tiles in every biome, built from the Aquatic Planet Pack's own worldgen data (backwall noise plus a per-biome element band). One row per biome family: a material dropdown offering that biome's solids, and a coverage text box (default 0.4), plus one starting-biome coverage (default 1) that applies to whatever biome the colony starts in. The Aquatic Planet Pack's own biomes are listed with their vanilla values as defaults. Feature rooms (carved caves, lakes, geodes), which the game's own pass leaves bare, get backwalls too (optional, on by default). A per-zone backwall census is logged at every world load. Surface and space subworlds stay open. New worlds only.
